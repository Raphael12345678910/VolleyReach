# VolleyReach MVP Walkthrough

Target length: 75-90 seconds

Open the presentation demo at `http://127.0.0.1:3000/?demo=1#/home` before recording.

## 0:00-0:10 — Problem and product

**Screen:** Home

**Narration:**

VolleyReach is a free recruiting workspace for boys volleyball athletes who may not have access to an expensive recruiting service. It organizes the work between deciding to play in college and contacting the right coaches.

## 0:10-0:24 — Complete athlete profile

**Screen:** My Profile; briefly open Athletics and Academics.

**Narration:**

Athletes first create one complete profile with academic information, volleyball measurements, film, coach contact details, and an optional tournament schedule. VolleyReach reuses these verified facts instead of making the athlete enter them repeatedly.

## 0:24-0:38 — Find legitimate programs

**Screen:** Find Schools, then College Directory.

**Narration:**

The school finder asks questions that directly affect the results, including division, region, contact availability, and list size. The directory currently contains 293 men's programs and 722 grouped coach contacts imported from a shared recruiting resource. Missing information is labeled rather than guessed.

## 0:38-0:55 — Build a researched email

**Screen:** Email Builder.

**Narration:**

Once the required profile steps are complete, the email builder turns those facts into a coach-ready introduction. The athlete must still add a school-specific research sentence, so every message stays personal rather than becoming spam.

## 0:55-1:07 — Track outreach

**Screen:** Outreach.

**Narration:**

The outreach tracker records saved programs, email status, sent totals, follow-up dates, and replies. Emails are sent from the athlete's own account, and VolleyReach keeps the process organized.

## 1:07-1:20 — Optional measurement tool

**Screen:** Jump Test; select Basketball Rim.

**Narration:**

The optional Jump Test estimates standing reach, approach touch, and vertical from a straight-on video using either a men's volleyball net or a regulation ten-foot basketball rim. The result is clearly labeled as an estimate until it is benchmarked against a Vertec.

## 1:20-1:30 — Testing and next step

**Screen:** Return Home.

**Narration:**

The MVP is now ready for structured athlete and coach testing. I will measure task completion, usefulness, confidence contacting coaches, weekly-use intent, and the product changes made from that feedback.
