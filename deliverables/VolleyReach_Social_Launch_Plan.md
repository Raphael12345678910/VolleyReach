# VolleyReach Social Launch

## Goal

Recruit the first 20 athlete testers and 3 school or club partners. Follower count is secondary to completed profiles, saved programs, and coach emails drafted or sent.

## Primary Channels

- **Instagram:** Reels, carousels, Stories, athlete reposts, and club collaborations
- **TikTok:** Short recruiting explanations and product demonstrations
- **Direct distribution:** Coach emails, team group chats, and athlete referrals

## Ready-to-Post Captions

### Launch Reel

Boys volleyball recruiting information is scattered across spreadsheets, athletics sites, email drafts, and notes. VolleyReach brings the process into one free workspace: build your athlete profile, find real men's programs, research schools, create personal coach emails, and track every follow-up. We are recruiting the first group of athlete and coach testers now.

**CTA:** Comment `TEST` or use the pilot link.

### Recruiting Profile Carousel

Before contacting a coach, make sure your profile includes your graduation year, position, height, approach touch, vertical, GPA, film, current coach contact, and upcoming club schedule when available. VolleyReach keeps those facts together and uses them to build a cleaner email draft.

**CTA:** Save this checklist, then complete your VolleyReach profile.

### Email Builder Walkthrough

VolleyReach writes the factual parts of your coach email from your completed profile. You still provide the most important sentence: why that specific school and volleyball program interest you. The result is faster than rebuilding every email and more personal than sending the same message everywhere.

**CTA:** Join the MVP user test and tell us what should improve next.

## Weekly Measurement

Track reach, saves, shares, profile visits, pilot signups, completed profiles, schools saved, emails drafted or sent, and partner referrals. Review results every Friday. Turn real pilot outcomes into the following week's content.
